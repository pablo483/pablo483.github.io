Multi-Tenant Inventory Management SaaS

A multi-tenant inventory platform designed for complete data isolation between organizations. Handles product catalogs, supplier workflows, stock transactions, and subscription plans with a scalable Spring Boot backend.

  • Production setup containerized with Docker Compose and Nginx load-balancing across 3 Spring Boot instances, benchmarked at 1,200 req/s using Grafana k6.
